Workflows

How each service gets done

Every service follows a clear routine, so you always know what happens next.

From a clear monthly plan to posts that go out on time.

  1. Plan

    Agree on your goals, audience and content pillars for the month.

  2. Create

    Design the graphics in Canva and write captions in your voice.

  3. Approve

    Send everything to you for review before anything goes live.

  4. Schedule

    Queue posts for the times your audience is most active.

  5. Review

    Check engagement each month and adjust the next plan.

Your inbox, calendar and files kept in order every week.

  1. Map

    List your recurring tasks, tools and the access I will need.

  2. Organize

    Set up inbox labels, folders and calendar rules that fit how you work.

  3. Handle

    Manage email, scheduling, data entry and follow-ups on a set routine.

  4. Track

    Log every task in a shared tracker so you can see progress anytime.

  5. Report

    Send a short weekly update on what is done and what is next.

From your ideal client profile to a verified, ready-to-use list.

  1. Define

    Set your ideal client by industry, role, location and company size.

  2. Source

    Find matching companies and decision-makers on LinkedIn and prospecting tools.

  3. Verify

    Check contact details, remove poor fits and clear out duplicates.

  4. Organize

    Deliver a clean list in Google Sheets or your CRM, with source links.

  5. Track

    Update lead status and notes so your follow-up stays on schedule.

Campaigns planned, built and checked before every send.

  1. Plan

    Set the goal, audience and single action each email should drive.

  2. Segment

    Group your list so each message reaches the right people.

  3. Create

    Write the subject line, copy and call to action, and design the layout.

  4. Test

    Check links, formatting and mobile view, then send a test.

  5. Analyze

    Review opens, clicks and replies, and improve the next campaign.

FAQ

Before we begin

How do we get started?

Send a short message or book a free call. We talk about what is taking your time, then I send a plan with the focus, hours and price for you to approve.

Which tools do you work in?

I work in the tools you already use, and I design graphics in Canva. Tell me what your business runs on during the call.

Will my information stay private?

Yes. I treat your accounts, files and client details as confidential, and I am happy to sign a confidentiality agreement if you need one.

What if I need more hours one month?

Message me and we will adjust. You can move up a plan or add hours for a busy season.

How will I know what you are working on?

You get a short weekly update on what is done and what is next, so you never have to chase progress.

How are the plans priced?

Simple monthly plans with a set number of hours, in US dollars: Starter $110, Growth $290 and Premium $580.

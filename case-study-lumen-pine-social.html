Case study · Social media management

Lumen & Pine on Instagram

How I would turn a quiet, product-only Instagram into a planned feed with helpful, personal and sales posts, all in one recognizable look.

Client
Online shop for hand-poured soy candles
Owner
Olivia Hartwell, CEO
My role
Virtual assistant, social media
Tools
Canva, Google Sheets, Meta Business Suite, Pinterest

The challenge

An account with no plan

Lumen & Pine had beautiful products, but its Instagram did not show that. These were the main problems.

Problem 1

No posting plan

Posts went up only when there was spare time: two or three a month, with weeks of silence in between.

Solved with: Strategy and calendar

Problem 2

Every post said "buy"

Almost every post was a product photo with "shop now", so followers had little reason to save, share or comment.

Solved with: Content pillars

Problem 3

A different look every time

Colors, fonts and photo styles changed from post to post, so the feed did not look like one brand.

Solved with: Post designs

Problem 4

Captions took an hour

Olivia wrote every caption from scratch, which made posting feel like a chore.

Solved with: Caption bank

What I built

A plan the team can follow

A written strategy, a working calendar in Google Sheets and a set of post designs in one consistent style.

The outcome

What Olivia gets

  • Three planned Instagram posts every week, ready a month ahead
  • Six content pillars, so the feed is helpful and personal, not only sales
  • One visual style that makes every post look like Lumen & Pine
  • A caption bank that turns an hour of writing into a few minutes of editing
  • A clear status for every post, from idea to posted
  • A monthly review of reach, saves, clicks and orders
